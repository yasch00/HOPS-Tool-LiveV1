Results

What the runs say so far.

Each finding below is one figure, one plain-language readout, and a link that reproduces it in the tool. Figures are regenerated with every model version; the stamp in the footer says when.

Finding 01

The threshold is geographic, not global.

Ranking the fleet by its Economic Decarbonization Threshold spreads plants across roughly 1.5–1.75 kg CO₂e/kg NH₃ on the LCOE basis in the current run (only 9 of 69 plants reach the conventional cost inside the sweep; the rest stay above it at every target), driven mainly by local renewable resource quality and delivered gas price rather than by technology cost, which is common across sites.

Readout to be finalised against the published run. No numbers on this page are placeholders for values that will not be sourced.

Finding 02

Below the threshold, cost is capital; above it, cost is fuel.

Decomposing levelized cost along the frontier shows the composition inverting: at business-as-usual carbon intensity the bill is gas and CO₂ handling, while at deep reduction it is electrolyser, renewable and storage capital. The crossover is where the two are comparable — which is why it moves with capex and gas price in opposite directions.

Finding 03

Hourly flexibility is what buys the first 20–40%.

In the optimal hybrid, the electrolyser follows the renewable profile and the reformer fills the gaps, with hydrogen storage absorbing the mismatch so the synthesis loop stays above its minimum load. Removing that flexibility — fixing the loop at constant rate — raises the threshold materially.
