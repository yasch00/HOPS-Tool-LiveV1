Citation, licence, contact.

HOPS is research software. It is free to use and free to check.

01 — How to cite

Cite the paper for results, the software for the tool.

Two citations, depending on what you used. Both are placeholders until publication; do not cite from this page in the meantime — email instead.

Paper

[AUTHORS] ([YEAR]). [TITLE]. [JOURNAL OR PREPRINT SERVER]. [DOI]

Software

[AUTHORS] ([YEAR]). HOPS: Hybrid Optimization of Plant Systems, version [0.0.0]. [REPOSITORY / ARCHIVE]. [DOI]

A BibTeX entry and a CITATION.cff file will be published with the code release.
