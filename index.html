Economic Decarbonization Threshold

How much ammonia carbon can you cut before it costs more?

HOPS co-sizes and hourly-dispatches an ammonia plant against any carbon-intensity target, then reports the point where a hybrid clean-and-fossil plant stops being the expensive option — with no subsidies applied.

01 — The question

Subsidy independence, not subsidy optimisation.

Most ammonia decarbonisation pathways are priced with 45V, 45Q or EU ETS support baked in, which makes the result a function of policy rather than of engineering. Strip the support out and a sharper question appears: for a given plant, in a given place, where do the unsubsidised cost curves actually cross?

Answering it needs hourly resolution. Electrolytic hydrogen is worth what the local wind and solar profile makes it worth in each of 8,760 hours; storage and Haber–Bosch turndown decide how much of that profile is usable. Annual averages hide exactly the effect that sets the threshold.

02 — The method

One mixed-integer program, sizing and dispatch together.

HOPS solves capacity and hourly operation in the same problem: renewables, electrolysis, steam methane reforming, carbon capture, air separation, hydrogen and ammonia storage, and Haber–Bosch synthesis. Capacities are endogenous; resource, price and demand series are exogenous. The carbon-intensity target enters as a constraint, so sweeping it traces the cost-versus-carbon frontier directly.

Endogenous

Unit capacities, hourly dispatch and ramping, storage state of charge, grid import/export, curtailment, on/off commitment of the reformer and synthesis loop.

Exogenous

Hourly wind and solar capacity factors, wholesale electricity and gas prices, plant nameplate and location, technology cost and performance, ammonia demand.

03 — What you can do with it

Four things the tool answers directly.

01

Find a plant's threshold

Pick any modelled plant and read its EDT, its cost curve, and the technology mix at the crossover.

02

Test a target

Set a carbon-intensity target and see what it costs, what gets built, and how the plant runs hour by hour.

03

Change an assumption

Move electrolyser capex, gas price or capture rate and watch the threshold move. Every default is sourced.

04

Compare geographies

Rank the fleet by threshold to see where unsubsidised hybrid ammonia lands first.

04 — The atlas

Every modelled plant, one view.

The atlas maps the ~91 modelled plants coloured by threshold, with each plant's resource profile, cost curve and optimal build behind a click. It is the fastest way to see that the answer is geographic, not global.
